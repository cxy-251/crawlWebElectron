# Character Base

Purpose:
- This folder is the identity anchor for the protagonist used across the whole poster series.
- All city posters should be generated from this same character base.

Folder usage:
- `reference-images/`: raw generated portraits for identity locking
- `approved/`: selected official character references
- `notes/`: extra notes about wardrobe, expression, and continuity

Character concept:
- A stylish young woman who feels intelligent, calm, curious, and emotionally self-possessed
- She travels alone, but the world around her feels cinematic and intimate
- She should look believable, aspirational, and high-end

Identity anchor:
- Female, 24-27 years old
- East Asian features
- Oval face
- Long dark brown hair
- Soft but defined jawline
- Clear skin with natural texture
- Expressive eyes
- Slim build
- Elegant posture
- Quiet confidence

Visual tone:
- Luxury fashion editorial
- Realistic photography
- Cinematic lighting
- Sophisticated styling
- No exaggerated makeup
- No overly sexualized posing

Master character prompt:
`same young East Asian woman, 25 years old, oval face, expressive almond-shaped eyes, clear natural skin texture, long dark brown hair, slim build, elegant posture, calm confident expression, luxury fashion editorial portrait, cinematic photography, subtle natural makeup, premium styling, realistic lighting, highly detailed, believable face identity`

Reference portrait prompt:
`a cinematic portrait of the same young East Asian woman, 25 years old, oval face, expressive almond-shaped eyes, long dark brown hair, natural skin texture, calm and intelligent gaze, wearing a minimal high-end neutral outfit, shot like a luxury fashion editorial, soft directional light, shallow depth of field, realistic photography, highly detailed, clean background, no text`

Reference generation advice:
- First generate close-up portraits with simple backgrounds
- Then select 2-4 images that define her identity best
- Reuse those selected references for every city poster
- Keep hairstyle changes subtle unless the story requires otherwise

Suggested negative prompt:
`blurry, plastic skin, cartoon look, exaggerated beauty filter, extra fingers, asymmetrical eyes, distorted face, bad anatomy, duplicate person, text, logo, watermark`

Poster text guidance:
- The character images in this folder should not contain text
- Text should be added later on the city posters
